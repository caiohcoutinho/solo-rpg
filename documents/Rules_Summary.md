# Passions

- Each Passion has a rating.
- A Passion can grant Pathos **once per scene**.

## Regaining Pathos

### Through Your Own Actions

- Perform an action that fulfills the Passion.
- Roll the Passion's rating (difficulty **6**).
- Each success restores **1 Pathos**, up to the normal maximum.

### Through Another Person's Emotions

- Be near a mortal experiencing the **exact same emotion in the same context** as the Passion.
- Roll the Passion's rating (difficulty **8**).
- Each success restores **1 Pathos**.

---

# Gaining Passions

Requires Storyteller approval and appropriate roleplay.

| Action | XP Cost |
|--------|--------:|
| New Passion | **3 XP** |
| Increase existing Passion | **Current rating × 3 XP** |

---

# Losing Passions

A Passion can be reduced in four ways.

## Neglect

If the Passion has been ignored for a long period:

- Roll the Passion's rating (difficulty **9**).
- **Failure:** Reduce the Passion by **1**.
- **Botch:** Reduce the Passion by **2**.
- If the Passion reaches **0**, immediately enter a **Harrowing**.

## Targeted Harrowings

A Targeted Harrowing may reduce or remove a specific Passion according to its own rules.

## Numbing

Certain attacks reduce a **random Passion** by **1**.

- If it reaches **0**, immediately enter a **Harrowing**.

## Resolution

See below.

---

# Weakening a Passion

Before attempting Resolution:

- Roleplay the character gradually letting go of the Passion.
- Roll the Passion's current rating (difficulty **9**).
- Each success reduces the Passion by **1**.
- A Passion cannot be reduced below **1** this way.
- As a guideline, reduce it to **at least half of its original rating** (rounded down, minimum **1**) before attempting Resolution.

---

# Resolving a Passion

When the Storyteller determines the character is ready:

- Roll **Willpower**.
- **Difficulty:** Original Passion rating + **5**.
- **Required successes:** Equal to the Passion's original rating.

## Results

| Result | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Success | Remove the Passion permanently. |
| Failure | Increase the Passion by **1**. |
| Botch | Enter a **Destruction Harrowing**. If survived, increase the Passion by **1** and this Passion can never be resolved again. |

---

# Restriction During Resolution

If the Passion is used to regain Pathos while attempting to resolve it:

- Resolution progress suffers a significant setback.
- Suggested rule: delay further Resolution attempts by a number of sessions equal to the Pathos gained.


# Fetters

## What Fetters Do

- A wraith with at least one Fetter may remain in the Shadowlands indefinitely.
- A wraith with no Fetters must spend **1 Willpower per scene** to remain in the Shadowlands.
- If they cannot pay, they are pulled into the nearest Byway.

## Recovering Willpower

### After interacting significantly with a Fetter

- Roll the Fetter's rating (difficulty **6**).
- Each success restores **1 Willpower**, up to the character's maximum.

### After 8 hours of Slumber in a Fetter

- Roll the Fetter's rating (difficulty **4**).
- Each success restores **1 Willpower**, up to the character's maximum.

---

# Losing Fetters

## Destruction

If a Fetter is destroyed (or a living Fetter dies):

- The wraith immediately enters a Harrowing.
- Remove the Fetter from the character sheet.

## Neglect

When the Storyteller determines a Fetter has been neglected:

- Roll the Fetter's rating (difficulty **9**).
- **Failure:** Reduce the Fetter's rating by 1.
- **Botch:** Reduce the Fetter's rating by 2.
- If the rating reaches **0**, the wraith immediately enters a Harrowing.

## Other Ways to Lose Rating

- Certain Harrowings can reduce or remove Fetters.
- **Rend the Lifeweb** reduces a Fetter's rating by 1.
- If the rating reaches **0**, the wraith immediately enters a Harrowing.

---

# Acquiring New Fetters

Requirements:

- Storyteller approval.
- Significant roleplaying establishing the attachment.

Cost:

- **3 × desired Fetter rating XP**
- The full cost must be paid at once.

---

# Resolving a Fetter

## Step 1: Weaken the Fetter

Once the Storyteller determines sufficient narrative progress has been made:

- Roll the current Fetter rating (difficulty **9**).
- Every **2 successes** reduce the Fetter's rating by **1**.
- The rating cannot be reduced below **1**.
- The Fetter must be reduced to **half its original rating (rounded down, minimum 1)** before a final Resolution attempt.

## Step 2: Resolution Roll

Roll:

- **Willpower**
- Difficulty = **5 + original Fetter rating**

To succeed:

- The number of successes must be **at least equal to the Fetter's original rating**.

### Success

- The Fetter is permanently Resolved.

### Failure

- Increase the Fetter's rating by **1**.
- More roleplaying is required before another attempt.

### Botch

- The Fetter is immediately destroyed.
- The wraith immediately undergoes a Destruction Harrowing.

> The Storyteller may award bonus dice for exceptional roleplaying.

---

# Benefits of Resolving Fetters

Each successfully Resolved Fetter brings the wraith one step closer to Transcendence.

If the wraith has **no remaining Fetters** but has **Resolved at least one**:

- They may remain in the Shadowlands by spending **1 Willpower per day** instead of **1 Willpower per scene**.

If a wraith Resolves **all** Fetters, the rules suggest they may be able to remain in the Shadowlands indefinitely, though this is presented as an extremely rare possibility rather than a guaranteed rule.

# Thorns

## Bad Luck
3 points — For each Angst point a Shadow with Bad Luck spends, the wraith’s player is forced to reroll one die (Shadowguide’s choice). The use of Bad Luck does not have to be announced until after the original roll is made.

## Honeyed Tongue
4 points — The Shadow is almost preternaturally persuasive, and it can really turn on the charm when it chooses to do so. By spending a point of Angst, and rolling temporary Angst (difficulty is the target’s Manipulation + Subterfuge), the Shadow can make any one sentence strangely compelling — and believable. This covers everything from "I’m sure Lucien won’t mind if you steal his relic beer" to "The Shade won’t bite if you just lean over and pet it nicely."

A wraith can spend a Willpower point to counteract the effects of this Thorn. Honeyed Tongue can be used only once per scene.
