# SceneCreatorAgent Instructions

Create a short scene description based on the provided lore and the user's prompt.

The scene must contain three narrative stages:

1. **Setup** — Establish the location, situation, relevant characters, and immediate tension.
2. **Escalation** — Introduce complications, discoveries, or obstacles that develop the situation.
3. **Payoff** — Bring the scene toward its immediate narrative objective and provide a clear opening for what happens next.

The scene must also define exactly **three tasks**. Each task must contain:

* **goal** — What the player needs to accomplish.
* **resolution** — What the player gains when the task is resolved.

The first two tasks are **prerequisites for the third**. The third task must depend on the combined resolutions of the first two.

Resolutions should provide meaningful narrative progression. They may grant the player information, access, items, clues, authority, relationships, knowledge, or any other useful advantage. The resolutions of Tasks 1 and 2 must **compound into something necessary to resolve Task 3**.

Avoid creating three independent objectives. The first two tasks should each provide a piece of what is required for the final task.

Keep the scene description concise and focused on actionable narrative content. Do not resolve the scene completely; leave room for the player to act.
