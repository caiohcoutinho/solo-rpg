**Art Guide – Monochrome Ethereal Horror Portraits (90s RPG NPC Style)**

This style aims to evoke the feeling of opening the dialogue portrait of an ancient ghost in a late-1990s dark fantasy RPG. The artwork should feel painterly rather than photographic, with stark contrast, restrained color, and an unsettling stillness instead of overt gore.

---

### Overall Mood

Create an image that feels silent, cold, supernatural, and melancholic. The horror should come from unnatural anatomy and impossible emptiness rather than violence or shock.

The subject should appear almost frozen in time, calmly staring at the viewer.

Avoid dramatic expressions. The stillness is what makes the portrait disturbing.

---

### Composition

Use a square composition.

Frame the subject from the shoulders upward.

The face should occupy approximately 60–70% of the canvas.

The head should be centered with nearly perfect symmetry.

Keep the background extremely simple and uncluttered.

Leave a generous amount of negative space around the silhouette.

---

### Color Palette

Use an almost monochromatic palette.

Primary colors:

* Pure black
* Charcoal gray
* Cold bluish gray
* Bright icy white
* Pale desaturated blue

The only noticeable color should be the cold blue tint in the background.

Never introduce warm colors.

Avoid reds, oranges, yellows, greens, pink skin tones, or saturated colors.

The contrast between the nearly black figure and the glowing blue-white background should be striking.

---

### Lighting

Use flat, diffuse lighting.

The subject should not appear lit by a visible light source.

There should be almost no highlights.

Shadows should merge naturally into the black silhouette.

The background itself should appear luminous, almost as if glowing through mist.

---

### Background

The background should be nearly white with a noticeable icy-blue tint.

Add subtle watercolor or paper texture.

Keep texture extremely faint.

No scenery.

No walls.

No fog effects.

No architecture.

No objects.

Nothing should distract from the face.

---

### Rendering Style

The image should resemble:

* charcoal drawing
* dry brush ink
* graphite illustration
* rough digital painting
* concept art
* hand-painted RPG portrait

Brushwork should remain visible.

Edges should feel organic.

Avoid clean vector lines.

Avoid smooth gradients.

Avoid airbrushed rendering.

Do not make the image look photographic.

---

### Face

Faces should be elongated slightly beyond normal human proportions.

Cheekbones should be pronounced.

The chin should be narrow.

The neck should be unnaturally thin.

The expression should remain neutral.

The lips should be closed.

No smile.

No scream.

No bared teeth.

The face should feel lifeless rather than emotional.

---

### Eyes

The eyes are the defining feature.

They should be completely hollow.

No eyeballs.

No pupils.

No iris.

No glowing effects.

The eye sockets should simply reveal the luminous background behind the figure.

The edges around the hollows should be rough black brush strokes.

The holes should read as impossible empty voids.

---

### Mouth

The mouth should remain naturally closed.

The lips should be dark and understated.

No teeth.

No tongue.

No screaming.

No exaggerated grin.

No distortion.

The mouth should contribute to the uncanny calmness.

---

### Hair

Hair should be:

* short
* shoulder-length or shorter
* wispy
* weightless
* gently floating

Individual strands should separate naturally.

Movement should be subtle, as though suspended underwater.

Avoid dramatic windswept hair.

Avoid perfectly groomed hairstyles.

Avoid shiny hair.

Hair should blend softly into the surrounding darkness.

---

### Body

Only show:

* neck
* collarbones
* tops of shoulders

The body should disappear naturally into darkness.

No clothing details unless specifically requested.

No jewelry.

No accessories.

No decorative elements.

---

### Texture

Faces should include:

* charcoal scratches
* dry brush texture
* uneven graphite marks
* rough painterly grain

The texture should remain visible at all scales.

Avoid perfectly smooth skin.

Avoid pores.

Avoid realistic skin rendering.

---

### Contrast

This style relies heavily on contrast.

The subject should be almost black.

The background should be nearly white with a blue tint.

The hollow eyes should immediately attract attention because they expose the bright background.

The image should read clearly even from a thumbnail.

---

### Inspiration

Take inspiration from:

* late-1990s RPG dialogue portraits
* gothic fantasy concept art
* monochrome horror illustrations
* ink wash paintings
* charcoal portrait studies
* dark fantasy bestiary illustrations

The result should feel like forgotten concept art from an old PC RPG rather than a modern horror movie still.

---

## Things the AI SHOULD do

* Keep the portrait centered.
* Maintain strong black-versus-white contrast.
* Use visible painterly brushwork.
* Make the eyes completely hollow.
* Use an icy blue-white background.
* Render with charcoal and dry-brush textures.
* Preserve a calm, supernatural expression.
* Create subtle asymmetries in texture while keeping facial proportions mostly symmetrical.
* Emphasize silence and unease over aggression.

---

## Things the AI should NOT do

* Do not create photorealistic faces.
* Do not use cinematic lighting.
* Do not add glowing pupils or supernatural irises.
* Do not make the ghost scream.
* Do not open the mouth.
* Do not expose teeth.
* Do not include blood, gore, wounds, or decay.
* Do not add heavy fog or elaborate visual effects.
* Do not use warm colors.
* Do not use saturated blues.
* Do not add clothing details, jewelry, ornaments, or backgrounds.
* Do not make the hair long, glamorous, or heavily windblown.
* Do not smooth away the brush texture.
* Do not create an anime, comic, or cartoon aesthetic.
* Do not make the portrait look like a digital photograph.
* Do not use high-detail skin textures or realistic facial pores.
* Do not introduce distracting elements that compete with the face.
